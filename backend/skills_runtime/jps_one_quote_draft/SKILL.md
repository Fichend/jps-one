# JPS One Quote Draft

Use this skill when a lead is interested or asks for price.

Inputs:

- Lead and company.
- Selected products and quantities.
- Currency.
- Customer level.
- Country and trade terms.

Output:

- Quote draft.
- Quote items.
- Total amount.
- MOQ, packing, lead time, payment terms.
- Approval requirement.
- Quote email body.

Rules:

- Quote history must be preserved.
- Prices are sensitive.
- Approval rules must be explicit.
- Do not silently overwrite quotes.
