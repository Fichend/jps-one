# Integrations

## Search

Primary customer discovery sources:

- Tavily
- Jina Reader
- Google Maps or map connectors
- public websites
- public industry directories
- B2B platforms
- user-uploaded files

## Meta / Facebook

Meta is not a full-platform customer search source.

Use it for:

- authorized business assets;
- Page sync;
- Lead Ads retrieval;
- messaging/profile supplement when permitted;
- diagnostics.

## Instagram

Requires professional account and linked Facebook Page. Treat as a future supplement, not a first-phase search source.

## WhatsApp

Use for follow-up support, templates, message state, and Webhook in later phases. Do not default to bulk auto-send.

## Email

SMTP and IMAP must be tested before sending or reply detection. First version defaults to draft plus human review.
